Key Findings

  • Risk classification accuracy is very close for the top three models (83.19–83.78%); Mistral trails at 81.85%.
  • Claude Sonnet-5 produces the most relevant article citations (F1 = 0.253) and the highest obligation coverage (74.10%).
  • GPT-5.4-mini is the fastest while tying for best minimal-risk recall and second-best obligation coverage.
  • Llama 3.1-70B-Instruct has the highest overall risk accuracy and the best limited-risk F1, but lower article and obligation scores.
  • Mistral 8B-2512 has the lowest article and obligation coverage and the lowest overall risk accuracy.

Conclusion

For this benchmark, Claude Sonnet-5 and GPT-5.4-mini provide the best balance of risk accuracy, citation quality, and obligation coverage. Llama 3.1-70B-Instruct is marginally best on raw risk accuracy but produces less complete citations and obligations. Mistral 8B-2512 is the least capable model on this task set.
